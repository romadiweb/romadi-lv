# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

ROMADI serves Latvian businesses and decision-makers who need a trustworthy partner for websites, e-commerce, digital product design, automation, growth, and long-term technical support. Visitors are usually evaluating capability, fit, and credibility before starting a conversation about a project.

## Product Purpose

The website presents ROMADI’s capabilities, demonstrates the quality of work the studio can deliver, and turns qualified interest into clear project enquiries. Success means a visitor quickly understands what ROMADI does, trusts the studio’s judgement, and can begin a useful conversation without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, premium, and approachable. ROMADI communicates with expert confidence, direct language, and a distinctive technical atmosphere without becoming cold, theatrical, or inaccessible.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing-page templates and interchangeable agency card grids.
- Bright magenta, pure crimson floods, or decorative neon effects that replace the established cherry-wine palette.
- Oversized, sparsely populated cards and sections with accidental empty space.
- Decorative glass, grids, borders, counters, or motion without a structural or interactive purpose.
- Corporate contact experiences that feel impersonal, bureaucratic, or needlessly long.

## Design Principles

- **Practice the promise.** Every page must demonstrate the clarity, polish, responsiveness, and technical quality ROMADI sells.
- **Make the next step obvious.** Visitors should always understand what to do and what will happen after they act.
- **Show expert confidence.** Use concise, specific copy and intentional hierarchy instead of inflated claims or visual noise.
- **Balance atmosphere with utility.** Signature motion and cherry-wine surfaces build recognition, while content and controls remain fast, readable, and direct.
- **Design for a real conversation.** Forms and contact paths should collect only information that helps ROMADI respond meaningfully.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across responsive web layouts. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and labels, readable validation and status messages, sufficient contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives for all non-essential animation.
